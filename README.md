# SJ Permit Helper: AI for struggle to find the right permits

Client: City of San José, Zoning & Leasing]
Team: [Elias Duong, Jovin Duong, Jose Garcia, Ishaan Pabla], Aug–Oct 2026
My part: I designed and presented the city staff view of the prototype, where staff review an applicant’s AI-generated permit steps and approve or send feedback.

## The problem
Small business owners in San José struggle to get clear, trustworthy answers about the permits, zoning rules, fees and other requirements that apply to them. Today they piece it together from several confusing city sources, guess at what applies, and lose confidence in the system before they ever reach the right desk.
## What we built
- What the prototype does: An app built on Replit that takes an applicant’s business type, location and what needs work in their leased space, and recommends the permitting steps that fit, routed through a fixed set of branches (leasing, zoning, infrastructure, food handling).
- What the user sees: A plain-language list of the permits and steps for their situation, which they can send straight to city staff instead of searching on their own.
- What a human still decides: City staff log in separately, review the AI’s recommended steps, add feedback, and give final approval
## What I would do next
Gap: City staff view has no real access control of the domain. Anyone can essentially see applicant's submissions and approve or reject permits without proper credentials.
Next build: Real authentication for staff accounts tied to the City's existing employee sign-in with role-based permissions so only authorized staff can review and approve
